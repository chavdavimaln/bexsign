# 06 — Sending Signatures Guide

## Recipient & Workflow Configuration
- **Recipient Roles**: Signer, Approver, Viewer, CC, Reviewer.
- **Authentication**: Email OTP option for enhanced security.
- **Signing Order**:
  - **Sequential**: Recipient 1 → Recipient 2 → Recipient 3.
  - **Parallel**: Simultaneous notification to all signers.
- **Automated Reminders**: Every 1, 3, 5, or 7 days.
- **Expiration Policies**: Document auto-expiration after specified days (default 30 days).
